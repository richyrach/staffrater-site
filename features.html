Staff ratings that tell you something.

Members choose a registered staff member, leave a one-to-five rating and can explain what happened. Profiles collect that history while safeguards stop one noisy person from defining somebody’s reputation.

Profiles and commentsView a staff member’s rating summary, rating count and recent written feedback.
One current voteEach member keeps one rating per staff member instead of stacking unlimited votes.
Edit and deleteMembers can correct or remove their own rating without creating duplicates.
Review safeguardsCooldowns pace repeated changes, and abusive or invalid ratings can be flagged for staff review.
Leaderboards and exportsShare server leaderboards or export rating data for deeper administrative review.

Support tickets

Create private support channels, let staff take responsibility and close each request with a reason when the work is done.

  • Choose the ticket category, support role and private log channel
  • Post a clear open-ticket panel for members
  • Private channel for the requester and support staff
  • Claim ownership and record a closing reason
  • Persistent buttons return after ordinary bot restarts

Staff applications

Ask the questions that matter to your server, review complete responses and record a clear decision.

  • Create multiple application forms with custom questions
  • Post an application panel in the channel you choose
  • Keep new responses in a pending staff queue
  • Review answers before accepting or rejecting
  • Delete forms or purge old responses when needed
