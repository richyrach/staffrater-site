Talk to our team
Who is Staff Rater for?

Discord communities that want to understand their staff’s work and organise support. Start with ratings and enable tickets, applications or activity tracking when your team needs them.

Who can access the dashboard?

Sign in with Discord. You’ll see servers you own where Staff Rater is installed. Members use the public panels and commands inside Discord.

Can one person ruin a staff member’s rating?

Each member keeps one current rating per staff member. Cooldowns pace repeated changes, suspicious patterns can be held for review, and weighted scores put small samples in perspective. Your managers keep the final judgment.

Do I have to configure everything?

No. Choose the features your community needs. The setup guide walks you through ratings first, then tickets and applications.
