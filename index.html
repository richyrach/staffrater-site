Before you start

A few useful answers.

Who can use the dashboard?

Sign in with Discord. The server picker shows servers you own that already have Staff Rater installed. Members submit ratings and use public panels inside Discord.

Do I need to enable every feature?

No. Start with ratings, add the staff members you want to make rateable, and connect the relevant channels. Configure tickets, applications or activity tools when you need them.

What does “owner only” mean on a command?

Some older messages mean the developer of Staff Rater, not the owner of your Discord server. Global bot diagnostics are restricted to the developers. Server-management commands use Discord permissions such as Manage Server.

Where can I report a problem?

Join the support server. Include the command or dashboard section, what you expected and what happened. Never share your bot token or account credentials.
